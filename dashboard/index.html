How the model works

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier × chain multiplier. The category multiple is the median mcap / TVL of the token-bearing protocols in the same category (at least 5 of them, or the median over all token-bearing protocols, ). The multiple is clamped to 0.03–1.00, so no category's thin data can price a protocol at ten times its TVL. Growth is the TVL change over 7 days, clamped to −60%…+120%, and moves the value by half its size. Maturity rises from 0.60 for a brand-new protocol to 1.00 at three years; a protocol with no listing date counts as one year old. Each chain beyond the first adds 3%, up to four extra chains. Values are rounded to whole dollars.

What this does not do: it does not predict whether or when a token is issued, and it does not use any name list — the same formula scores every row. Market cap in the snapshot is circulating market cap, not fully diluted value, so the implied value is a circulating-cap estimate. A protocol that records no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are not launch candidates and are left off the board: a protocol whose category is Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC or CEX, whose name carries a whole word wrapped, staked or staking, or whose name begins with an exchange's name is not ranked. The watchlist itself passes that rule already, so the floor is untouched.
