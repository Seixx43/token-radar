Which pre-token protocol would be worth the most if it launched a token today?

Every protocol below records no token in the DefiLlama snapshot. Each one is scored by its own figures — TVL, the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the token-bearing protocols in its own category, and the launch odds its age band earns from the peers' record — and each farm plan opens with a verdict: worth farming, farm small, or wait, from those same odds and the gas the whole farm costs. The verdict prices gas, so no Ethereum protocol earns the top verdict: a weekly action there costs about $6, and even the shortest farm runs about $34 of gas against a $50–$250 position, while the same farm on a cheap chain costs $9–$26. That is the verdict working as designed — the chain is part of the farm's cost, and the top verdict is reserved for farms where the gas does not eat the position. The board is a claim about value, not a prediction that a token is coming.

How the model works

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × launch odds. The category multiple is the median mcap / TVL of the token-bearing protocols in the same category (at least 5 of them, or the median over all token-bearing protocols, ). The multiple is clamped to 0.03–1.00, so no category's thin data can price a protocol at ten times its TVL. The launch odds are the share of the snapshot's protocols in the same age band that still record no token — the peers' record on whether a launch is still coming: under one year most peers have no token yet, while at three years or more 79% already have one, so a protocol that has run that long with no token is the exception. A protocol with no listing date takes the oldest band's odds. Values are rounded to whole dollars.

What this does not do: it does not predict whether or when a token is issued, and it does not use any name list — the same formula scores every row. Market cap in the snapshot is circulating market cap, not fully diluted value, so the implied value is a circulating-cap estimate. A protocol that records no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are not launch candidates and are left off the board: a protocol whose category is Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC or CEX, whose name carries a whole word wrapped, staked or staking, or whose name begins with an exchange's name is not ranked. The watchlist itself passes that rule already, so the floor is untouched. The 7-day TVL change and the chain count are shown on the board as facts, but they are not model inputs: tested held-out on the 128 token-bearing protocols, neither moves the ranking, and neither does a fitted log-linear regression, whose coefficients flip sign between folds. The board also flags its own shaky numbers: , and each is marked on the board and in its detail view, because an implied value built on a TVL that just jumped or collapsed deserves a warning, not a quiet number — the flag never changes the ranking, since the snapshot gives no evidence for how much to discount a moved TVL.

The farm plan's numbers are design choices, not fitted from the snapshot, and they are stated here so they can be judged as such. The hold runs 60 days for a protocol under six months old, 45 days under a year, 30 days otherwise — a younger protocol has less history, so the plan gives it more weeks of qualifying activity. The capital scales with TVL ($50 under $100M, $100 to $1B, $150 to $5B, $250 above) so the position stays small relative to the protocol. The top verdict needs the whole farm's gas at or under $30: above that the gas eats a small position, so the verdict drops a tier — which is why no Ethereum protocol earns it. The gas itself is approximate (about $6 a weekly action on Ethereum, $1–$2 elsewhere), so the verdict is a call on the order of magnitude, not a quote.
